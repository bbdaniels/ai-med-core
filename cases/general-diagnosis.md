# General Medical Diagnosis Case Template

Use this template to generate vignettes for general medical interview training.
Suitable for any clinical specialty -- adapt sections as needed for the specific case type.

---

## Structure

### Case Title
A descriptive title summarizing the clinical scenario.

### Patient Background
- **Name**: Full name (culturally appropriate for the setting)
- **Age / Gender**
- **Occupation / Living Situation**
- **Marital / Family Status**
- **Socioeconomic Context**
- **Setting**: Where the visit takes place
- **Primary Reason for Visit**: One-line reason
- **Lifestyle / Supports**: Diet, exercise, substance use, social context

### Chief Complaint & Opening Statement
A first-person quote the patient says when greeting the doctor. Should be natural
and conversational -- how a real patient would describe why they're here.

### History of Present Illness
- Onset, duration, course
- Character and severity of symptoms
- Associated symptoms
- Aggravating and relieving factors
- Impact on daily function
- Prior episodes or treatments tried

### Past Medical History
Bullet list of conditions.

### Medications
Bullet list with doses.

### Allergies

### Family History

### Social History

### Review of Systems (Focused)
Only systems relevant to the case.

### Provider Questions and Scripted Responses
10-15 question-answer pairs covering the clinical interview. Write in natural
patient language. Include:
- Questions about the chief complaint
- Questions about associated symptoms
- Questions the patient would answer "no" or "I don't know" to
- At least one question where the patient gives a vague or incomplete answer

### Physical Examination Findings
Organized by system. Include:
- Vitals
- Relevant positive findings
- Relevant negative findings (pertinent negatives)
- Any diagnostic test results mentioned in the scenario

### Assessment / Differential Diagnosis
3-5 prioritized diagnoses with brief rationale.

### Management Plan
Bullet list of recommended actions.

### Counseling & Education Points
Key topics to discuss with patient.

### Emotional & Behavioral Cues
- Tone, affect, physical mannerisms
- A spontaneous comment that reveals character

### Learning Objectives
3 bullet points describing what the student should practice/learn.

---

## Generation Notes

- Patient responses should sound natural, not clinical
- Include realistic details that make the patient feel like a real person
- Physical exam findings should be internally consistent with the history
- The case should have a clear primary diagnosis but reasonable differential
- Difficulty level should match the intended learner (student, resident, etc.)
