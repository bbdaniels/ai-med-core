# Case Title

Synthetic Demo Case: Elbow Discomfort After Heavy Keyboard Use

**THIS CASE IS FAKE.** Every detail below was invented to give this repository a
working example that a developer can run end to end. It is not a real patient, it
is not derived from a real encounter, and it is not medical advice or clinical
training material. Do not reuse it for teaching or assessment. Replace it with
your own IRB-approved or curriculum-approved case content before fielding

## Patient Background

Alex Rivera, 34 years old, works as a software developer at a small company and
has done so for eight years. Lives in a rented apartment with one roommate.
Unmarried, no children. Walks to work most days. No caregiving responsibilities.
Emergency contact is a sibling who lives in the same city.

## Chief Complaint

"My right elbow has been aching for about three days."

**Scripted opening statement:** "Hi, thanks for seeing me. My right elbow has been
aching for the last three days or so, and it is starting to bug me while I work."

## History of Present Illness

The ache started roughly three days ago, the morning after a long stretch of work
finishing a project deadline. Alex estimates ten to twelve hours at the keyboard
that day, which is more than usual. The discomfort sits on the outer side of the
right elbow. It is dull rather than sharp, comes on during typing and gripping,
and settles within an hour of stopping. Rated 3 out of 10 at its worst, 0 at rest.

There has been no injury, no fall, no blow to the arm, and no sudden onset. No
swelling, no redness, no warmth, no bruising. No locking or giving way of the
joint. No numbness or tingling in the hand or fingers. No neck pain. No weakness
gripping a coffee cup, though gripping does reproduce the ache. Nothing has been
tried for it yet apart from taking a few short breaks from typing, which helped a
little. Symptoms are not worse at night and do not wake Alex from sleep.

## Past Medical History

No chronic conditions. No prior surgery. No previous problems with either elbow,
shoulder, or wrist. No history of inflammatory joint disease. Immunizations up to
date. Last routine visit was roughly a year ago and was unremarkable.

## Medications

- Ibuprofen 200 mg daily as needed, taken perhaps twice in the last six months,
  most recently for a headache about two months ago.
- No prescription medications.
- No supplements or herbal products.
- No known drug allergies.

## Provider Questions and Scripted SP Responses

1. "Can you tell me what brings you in today?"
   "My right elbow has been aching for about three days. It started after a really
   long day at the keyboard."

2. "Where exactly does it hurt?"
   "On the outside of the elbow, right about here. It is more of a dull ache than
   a sharp pain."

3. "Did you injure it, or did it come on gradually?"
   "No injury at all. I did not fall or hit it. It just crept up on me."

4. "What makes it worse?"
   "Typing, mostly. And gripping things. It fades out about an hour after I stop."

5. "How bad is it, on a scale of zero to ten?"
   "Maybe a three when I am working. Zero when I am resting."

6. "Any swelling, redness, or warmth around the joint?"
   "No, nothing like that. It looks completely normal to me."

7. "Any numbness or tingling in your hand or fingers?"
   "No, none at all."

8. "Have you had this before?"
   "Never. This is the first time either elbow has bothered me."

9. "Have you tried anything for it?"
   "Just taking short breaks from typing. That seems to help a bit."

10. "How is it affecting your work or daily life?"
    "It is not stopping me doing anything, it is just distracting when I am trying
    to concentrate."

## Physical Examination Findings

examination, answering as the nurse.

- **Vitals:** BP 118/76, heart rate 68 and regular, temperature 36.8 C,
  respiratory rate 14, oxygen saturation 99% on room air.
- **Inspection of right elbow:** no swelling, no erythema, no deformity, no
  visible asymmetry compared with the left.
- **Palpation:** mild tenderness over the lateral aspect. No warmth. No effusion.
- **Range of motion:** full and pain-free in flexion, extension, pronation, and
  supination.
- **Strength:** 5/5 grip bilaterally. Discomfort reproduced on resisted wrist
  extension on the right.
- **Neurovascular:** sensation intact throughout the right hand, radial pulse
  normal, capillary refill under two seconds.
- **Left elbow:** entirely normal, examined for comparison.

## Assessment

For the purposes of this synthetic demo, the picture is a three-day history of
activity-related lateral elbow discomfort in a well 34-year-old, following an
identifiable increase in repetitive keyboard use, with a normal examination apart
from mild lateral tenderness and discomfort on resisted wrist extension. There are
no systemic features and no neurovascular findings.
